P2P Foundation

AI tools for the commons

A set of AI-assisted research tools built over the P2P Foundation Wiki’s 45,000 articles — so that two decades of accumulated work on commons, peer production and cosmo-localism can be searched, summarised and translated by anyone.

In development

What is planned

  • Semantic search across the whole wiki, not just title matching
  • On-demand translation of any article, already running on the wiki itself
  • Summaries and reading paths through long-running topics
  • Self-hosted models, so that reading the commons does not require handing it to anyone
